# CivicSense AI
### From Complaints to Intelligent Action

**An AI-Powered Civic Complaint Management & Intelligence Platform**

CivicSense AI is a smart civic technology solution developed for **PromptWars x ASTRA – AI for Civic Impact**.

The platform aims to transform traditional public complaint management into an intelligent, transparent and action-oriented process.

Citizens can report civic problems such as damaged roads, waterlogging, overflowing garbage, broken streetlights and drainage issues. The system analyzes submitted complaints, identifies potential duplicates, assigns explainable priorities and helps authorities identify problems requiring attention.

## Key Features

- **Smart Complaint Reporting:** Submit civic issues with descriptions, images and location information.
- **AI-Powered Analysis:** Automatic complaint categorization and summarization.
- **Duplicate Detection:** Identify potentially repeated complaints.
- **Intelligent Prioritization:** Classify complaints based on urgency, safety risk and community impact.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Visualize complaint statistics and trends.
- **Geographic Hotspot Detection:** Identify areas experiencing recurring civic problems.
- **Administrative Workflow:** Review, assign and manage reported issues.
- **Citizen Tracking:** Monitor complaint progress from submission to resolution.

## How It Works

Citizen Reports Issue → AI Analyzes Complaint → Priority Assigned → Dashboard Reveals Insights → Authority Takes Action → Citizen Tracks Resolution

Our mission is to bridge the communication gap between citizens and civic authorities through intelligent automation, data-driven decision-making and transparent complaint tracking.
